Pet Cards Guide
🐾
How Pet Cards Work
Mining, upgrades, storage & pricing — everything in one place
💰 Passive Mining
Every pet you buy adds its Speed to your Total Speed (shown as Coins/hr at the top of this page). That speed accrues automatically into your Storage every second — even while you're away from the app.
When you buy any pet, it starts at Level 1 and begins mining immediately — right after the purchase, with no extra step needed.
⬆️ Upgrading Pets
Each pet can be upgraded up to Level 100. Every level permanently adds +10% of that pet's base Speed. Each upgrade costs 10% of the pet's original purchase price — the same cost at every single level.
📦 Storage
Storage has a limited capacity that caps how long coins can accrue before you claim them: 6h → 8h → 12h → 16h → 24h (5 levels). Upgrading Storage costs a flat 300,000 Coins per level.
Claim regularly — coins earned beyond your Storage capacity are lost if you wait too long to claim.

Dancing Bear gains an extra +0.5% of its base Speed automatically every day at 00:00 UTC, up to a maximum of +10% total (20 days) — completely free, no action needed.

How to unlock the free exclusive pets:
• Happy Dog — invite 100 active friends.
• The Guardian — watch 4,000 ads in total (any ad watched anywhere in the bot counts toward this).
• The Ambassador — reserved only for users with active channels who have collaborated with us. Contact support for more information.
💡 Tips
  • Higher-tier pets give more Speed per Coin spent — save up before buying if you can.
  • Upgrade your highest-Speed pet first for the biggest boost per Coin spent on upgrades.
  • Upgrade your Storage capacity early so you don't lose coins while you're away.
  • Check the Exclusive Cards — some pets can be unlocked for free instead of bought.
